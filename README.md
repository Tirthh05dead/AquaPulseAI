# 🌊 AquaPulse AI

## Smart Water Intelligence & Sustainability Command Center

AquaPulse AI is an AI-powered water monitoring and decision-support platform developed as a Research Paper → Prototype project.

The system transforms traditional water-quality monitoring into a smart-city intelligence platform capable of contamination-risk assessment, sustainability analytics, and citizen-impact monitoring.

---

## Problem Statement

Water contamination and distribution issues often remain undetected until they impact communities.

Traditional monitoring systems focus on collecting data but provide limited decision-support capabilities.

---

## Solution

AquaPulse AI combines:

* AI-powered contamination prediction
* Smart water network visualization
* Sustainability analytics
* Citizen impact monitoring
* Water demand forecasting
* Decision-support recommendations

into a unified dashboard.

---

## Key Features

### 🤖 AI Risk Prediction

Estimates contamination probability based on water quality indicators.

### 🌍 Smart Water Network

Visualizes monitoring zones and contamination hotspots.

### 📊 Analytics Dashboard

Provides water quality trends and operational insights.

### 🔮 Demand Forecasting

Predicts future water demand for proactive resource planning.

### ♻ Sustainability Intelligence

Tracks conservation impact and SDG alignment.

### 👥 Citizen Impact Engine

Estimates population exposure and public health impact.

### 🏙 Digital Twin Monitoring

Provides a city-wide operational view of water infrastructure.
